Security awareness starts with everyday decisions: whether to trust a message, share a document, or approve an unexpected request. These trainings focus on recognising risks and choosing a safe next step without requiring a technical background.

## Training topics

- Recognising phishing messages and social engineering tactics across email, messaging, and phone calls.
- Protecting accounts with unique passwords, password managers, and multi-factor authentication.
- Handling sensitive information and checking access before sharing files.
- Verifying unusual requests through a separate, trusted communication channel.
- Reporting suspicious activity promptly, including when a link has already been clicked or information shared.

## Approach

Realistic scenarios connect security principles to common workplace situations. The emphasis is on practical decisions: what looks suspicious, how to verify a request, and when to ask for help.

## Focus

Build repeatable habits rather than relying on memorised rules. A clear reporting process and a blame-free approach help people raise concerns early.
